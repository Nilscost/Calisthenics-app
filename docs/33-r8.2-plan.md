# 33 · R8.2 plan (owner feedback, 2026-10-17)

Start this only after R8.1 is released (doc 31, T13). It ships as **`0.11.2-r8`, versionCode 16**. Rules and workflow are the same as doc 31: commit prefix `R8.2-TNN:`, verify in Docker, ui-screens review, progress notes, no phone claims, content DRAFT in the docs only.

## G1 Power exercises go into their movement categories (no Power tab)
- Lower-body jumps and hops (the `plyo`, `plyo-lateral` and `plyo-box` families) move into the **Squat / legs** tab as their own power routes, next to the strength routes. Jump squat sits with squat; bounds and single-leg hops with lunge/single-leg work. Box jumps and depth jumps form the box route.
- Power push-ups stay in **Push**. Any upper-body plyo, such as the incline push-up depth jump, goes to Push.
- Remove the Power tab. Keep the R8 gates: the `squat-air` / `pushup-standard` achievements, the 3-rep cap on the six explosive push-ups, and depth jumps locked until box jumps are solid.
- Power routes stay out of the default automatic pool; they are added manually in Preview.

## G2 Prehab merges into a "Warm-up" category
- The prehab families (shoulder, wrist, hip, knee, neck) and the existing warm-up items together become one **Warm-up** category in Progress/search, with subgroups by body area.
- **Automatic warm-up:** when Warm-up is on (G3), pick 2–4 items matched to the session's main exercises, for example:
  - wrist prep for handstand, planche or push-up;
  - band external rotation / YTW for dips or HSPU;
  - Spanish squat or knee items for pistol or sissy;
  - hip stability for single-leg work.

  Keep the warm-up within its current time budget.

## G3 Train setup: one horizontal "Include" row
- Next to **Between sets** and **Workout format**, add a third horizontal control: **Include**, with three toggle buttons, **Warm-up**, **Conditioning** and **Cool-down**. A button is gold (accent, filled) when that part is in the workout, and outlined when it isn't.
- This replaces the current warm-up and cool-down switches. Keep the existing cool-down modes (stretching / timed) inside its option if they exist today.
- **Conditioning** = an optional finisher of 5–10 min at the end: a short circuit from the Conditioning category (burpee ladder, jacks, mountain climbers, jump rope, agility, crawls) at the person's level, Flow on by default, filtered by equipment. Conditioning stays its own category in Progress.
- The Preview shows the included parts as blocks, editable like everything else (F6 persistence applies).

## G4 Remove "exercises in each set" at the bottom of the Train page
It duplicates the "Exercises per round" stepper. Remove that control and keep the stepper. If both controls write to the same setting, remove only the UI. If they don't, migrate the stored value so it behaves like the stepper.
