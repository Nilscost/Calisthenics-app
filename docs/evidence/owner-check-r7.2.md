# Owner check, release R7.2 (0.10.2-r7, versionCode 12)

Nothing here was tested on a phone: this list is what you walk through on the Galaxy S21. All exercise content is an unreviewed DRAFT.
Install `app-debug-R7.2.apk`. The shoulder pass-through and Pallof clips are new in this APK (rendered on your Mac).
Each step names the feedback item it checks (F = your feedback on 0.10.1-r7, O = your answer).

## 1. Preview
1. Train > Workout preview. The round view shows **"All sets · N min each"** and "Changes apply to all sets". There are **no** Set 1 / Set 2 tabs. Open "Full timeline": the set numbers appear there as separators (F1).
2. Swap an **inverted row** (swap icon on its tile). Tap **Other types**: it turns into a header with an arrow, the sheet scrolls down to the new list, and the first group is **"Same family: Row"** in a light gold box, with the kettlebell and dumbbell rows. Every other type (Push, Pull, Squat, Hips and back, Core, Lower legs) is still listed below it, plain (F2, F3, O3).
3. Between two exercises: the stretch or rest is a **boxed row with the same swap icon** as the tiles (no word "change"). The **+** is on its own line below the box, at the right, with a clear gap (F4).

## 2. Cool-down
4. Switch on Cool-down (Stretching) on Train, open the Preview. Remove one stretch with its **x**; remove another with a **long press**. Tap **+** after the last card, pick a stretch: it appears at the end. The minutes follow. Save the workout as a routine and open it again: your changes are still there (F5).

## 3. Workout
5. Start a workout, tap Pause: **Resume** is the **gold filled** button on **one line**. Try it with the system font size at the largest step too (F6).
6. On a rep exercise the number reads **"8 x"**, on a hold **"30 s"** (F12). The same units are in "Edit what I logged" on the end screen and when you correct a workout in History.

## 4. Progress and History
7. Mark one exercise "Too easy" in a workout at **level 3** and finish it: Progress shows **"Move on to <exercise> level 5?"**. Accept it: levels 3 and 4 count as done (stars). At **level 4** the card says **"Move on to <next exercise>?"** and accepting starts the next exercise at level 1. At level 1 the card offers level 3 (F7, O1). Nothing changes until you tap Move on; closing the card brings it back with the next "Too easy".
8. The exercise detail no longer shows "Too easy: n of 2" (F7).
9. History > Progressions > open a card: the chart has a **short date under the dots** (every dot up to 6; with more, every second one and always the newest) (F8).
10. Planche lean: it shows "needs Plank" **only while your plank is below level 4**. If your plank is level 4 or more, nothing is shown, and that is correct (F9, O2; no code change in this release).

## 5. Clips
11. Clear the app cache (Settings > Apps > the app > Storage > Clear cache), then check the **shoulder band pass-through** (straight arms, visible band, hips to overhead to behind the back and back) and the **Pallof press / hold** (hands at the sternum, press straight forward, band pulling to the side). Known: in the pass-through the behind-the-back part may read poorly (one arm sticks out sideways); tell me if it does (F10, F11).
