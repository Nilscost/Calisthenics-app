# Owner check · R8.1 (0.11.1-r8, versionCode 15)

This is the phone walk-through for the new version. Automated checks and emulator screenshots do not cover real hands, voice, haptics or workout comfort, and nothing here has been tried on a phone. Exercise content remains DRAFT. Install `app-debug-R8.1.apk` as an update over R8 (same signing certificate); export a backup from Settings first.

1. **F1 Level after related work.** Train as usual (full body, Home profile). With feet-elevated push-ups at 4 stars and no chair in the profile, the push-up slot must show diamond (or feet-elevated if you add a chair), never knee or standard. If you have done split squats, the squat slot must start at split squat or above, not at assisted or air squat. If you imported a backup, check the same.
2. **F2 One exercise per progression.** Open Preview for a full-body and for a focus session with a high "exercises per round". No two exercises from the same progression (for example knee and standard push-up). Add one by hand with the + : that one is allowed.
3. **F3 Actual reps.** In a workout log fewer reps than prescribed (for example 6 of 10 on inverted rows) on the first set. The next set must start at 6. Finish and open History > Progressions: the dot and "best" show 6 (or what you logged), not 10. Tap Skip after typing a number: the set is kept as partial with that number. Try the editor on the end screen.
4. **F4 Level-up rule.** Do three good sessions of an exercise within two weeks (for example every 4 to 5 days): its level should rise after the third. Two sessions, or three spread over three weeks, should not raise it. A "Too hard" or pain session does not count. Sessions on feet-elevated and diamond count together.
5. **F5 Set formats.** In Preview, switch Straight sets / Pairs / Circuit and the number of sets, for a generated workout, a ready-made routine and a program. The order of the exercises changes with the format.
6. **F6 Edits survive.** In Preview change a level, swap an exercise, add and remove one, change a rest, a stretch, the sets, the format and Flow. Go back to Train, open Preview again: everything is still there. Pick another goal, routine or program on Train: the Preview starts clean.
7. **F7 Flow.** In Preview switch Flow on: no rest between exercises, the next one starts after the reps or the time; the rest between sets is unchanged. Do a short Flow workout and check that the reps you log still count, and that the end screen editor can correct a number.
8. **F8 Stretches between sets.** In a long workout with stretching on, no stretch comes up more than twice; when there are none left the break is plain rest.
9. **F9 Programs and routines.** On Train, Objective > Routine: one dropdown with Programs (ready-made routines and the Army PRT and Pilates mat sessions) and My routines. Pick Army PRT: it opens in Preview as a list. Change a target, remove an item, add one, switch Flow, then save it as a new routine: it appears under My routines and the program itself is unchanged.
10. **F10 No draft bubble.** The Preview, the Progress exercise sheet and Settings no longer say "unreviewed draft".
11. **F11 Long sessions.** Army PRT shows about 37 minutes and Pilates mat about 33 minutes in Train and in Preview.
12. **F12 Progress search.** The search field is the first control on Progress, above By type / By skill; there is no suggestion card.
13. **F13 Alternatives.** In Progress > Push, the diamond push-up is on the screen right next to feet-elevated, marked "other way". Look at the squat, pull-up and row routes too: other ways sit beside their rung. Support drills are still under Helps with.
14. **F14 Equipment.** In onboarding and in a profile, the equipment list is in groups (Pull, Push, Legs, Core, Weights, Other) and every row has an icon. Settings profile summaries use the same order.
15. **F15 Plurals.** A workout with one round says "1 round" in History.

At the end, give each main tab (Train, Progress, History, Settings) a score from 1 to 5, one thing that works well and one thing to change. Phone results and owner acceptance remain open until this walk-through is done.
