# Final owner checklist: one walk-through of the whole app (version 2, release R6, 0.9.0-r6)

Install `Apps/builds/app-debug-R6.apk` over the existing app. This merges `owner-check-r1.md` to `owner-check-r6.md`, in the order you would meet the screens. Nothing here was tested on a phone by anyone; everything is an unreviewed draft (exercises, numbers, stretches). Weak clips are listed in `docs/evidence/clips-v3-review.md`.

## 1. First start and questionnaire (R3)
- Goal page: Body part / Skill / Routine buttons and a dropdown; header GOAL · EQUIPMENT · LEVEL; equipment page (new options: dip support, foot anchor, barbell, weighted vest); level pages show the whole progression of a family with pictures and five levels (pull page has the flexed-arm hang); "You are ready" shows stars (a start at level 3 = two stars).
- Is any question missing or confusing? Redo it from Settings.

## 2. Train (R3, R4, R5, R6)
- Objective: Body part, Skill, **Routine** (Recommended Routine, Minimalist Routine, Starting To Stretch and its halves, then your own). Equipment dropdown and pencil. Workout format: Circuit / Pairs / Straight sets. Sets or rounds, Between sets (Stretch or Rest), **Warm-up first**, the time and exercise count, START WORKOUT, Workout preview.
- Does anything you used to do on Train seem missing (Reps or Timed moved to Settings)?

## 3. Round preview (R4)
- Per exercise a level stepper (− / +), a swap sheet (same movement plus "Other types"), "+" between cards to add an exercise or stretch, long-press to remove, a stretch or rest row you can change. **Detailed edit**: every set listed, a stretch per set, a typed number that does not count. **Progression rule**: App levels, Rep range (3 × 5–8, holds 10–30 s), Custom. **Save as routine** / Update this routine.
- Do the numbers and the 75-minute Recommended Routine (with warm-up) feel right? Is the Pairs preview (set tabs) clear?

## 4. Workout (R1, R2, R4, R5, R6)
- Clip on white above a dark sheet with the timer, exercise, set, Pause · DONE · Skip. Headers: ROUND 2 OF 4, PAIR 1 · SET 2 OF 3, EXERCISE 2 · SET 1 OF 3, STEP n OF N. The clip changes at every block. During recovery a number logger (− number +) folds the logged value; DONE ends a set.
- Warm-up blocks say "raise / activate / mobilise"; the stretch session says "ease in and out 10 times" and "Hold for 20 seconds". Readable from 2 m? Voice and sounds? Screen off?

## 5. End screen and History (R1, R2, R5)
- End screen: "Edit what I logged", a number per set, Too hard · Too easy · Pain. History: three tiles (sessions this week, level-ups this month, minutes), the week strip, workouts, **Progressions** with the lowest-set chart and the dashed gate line, a workout's detail with per-round corrections ("corrected"). Do your stars follow corrected numbers?
- Two "Too easy" in a row: a **Move up** suggestion appears in Progress; nothing changes until you tap it.

## 6. Progress (R3, R5, R6)
- By type | By skill; tabs Push, Shoulders, Pull, Row, Squat, Hips and back, Core, With weights, Lower legs, Stretches. Tiles: level number, picture, stars; first tap = panel, OPEN EXERCISE DETAIL = clip, body figure, five levels, **Weight** section for kettlebell and dumbbell exercises, unlocks, needs, cautions. A **suggestion card** (one at a time, can be closed). Stretches list with 24 stretches.
- Is the tree readable? (The Pull tab is busy.) Do exercises below your level show five stars?

## 7. Settings
- Equipment profiles (Home, Travel, new ones), Rounds, Reps/Timed, Stretch between exercises, voice and sound, **Automatic progression** switch (off = nothing moves), Redo the questionnaire, theme (System / Light / Dark), Backup export and import (the file carries round corrections and saved routines), privacy.

## 8. Look and feel (R2, R6)
- Dark by default, gold buttons and active tab, Barlow Condensed titles and Plex text. The white frame around clips on dark screens, the 4:3 clip not full-bleed: acceptable?

## 9. Content questions
- Safety copy: door/towel row, Nordic couch anchor, chair dips (depth cap, shoulder pain), neck in the head-supported bridge, barbell. Clear, not too scary?
- Are the clips understandable enough (weak: calf and tibialis raises, L-sit steps, wall walk-down, towel row, pull-up hangs)?
- Not added on purpose: Jefferson curl and German hang (risky; the opt-in flag exists). Want them?

Not checked by anyone: how it feels on the phone, haptics, sound, voice, screen-off behaviour, battery.
