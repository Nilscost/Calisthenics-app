# 9.3 L3: "mastered but still planned" (archer push-up)

Question from doc 43 §1 "also seen": in the owner's screenshots the archer push-up is "Mastered" and was still in the workout, although 9.2 made the
half one-arm push-up the next step (`pushup-archer` to `pushup-half-one-arm` to `pushup-one-arm-elevated`, route `pushup`).

Verdict: **intended, no bug, no planner change.**

## What the code does

Test: `domain/src/commonTest/kotlin/app/calisthenics/domain/MasteredStillPlannedTest.kt` (7 tests, all pass on the unchanged engine and planner).

| History (archer push-up) | Fifth star | `activeInFamily` | Planned push-ups (3, 4, 6 rounds, route counts 0..7) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hand level 5, three good sessions at level 5 | yes | `pushup-half-one-arm` | half one-arm only |
| level editor "all five done" (stored 6, `ALL_TIERS_DONE`) | yes | `pushup-half-one-arm` | half one-arm only |
| editor "levels 1 to 4 done", three sessions at level 5 | yes | `pushup-half-one-arm` | half one-arm only |
| hand level 3, then three good sessions each at 3, 4 and 5 | yes | `pushup-half-one-arm` | half one-arm only |
| plain hand-set level 5, no sessions | no (4 stars, `achievedTiers` empty) | `pushup-archer` | archer (and its alternate) |
| hand level 5, two good sessions at level 5 (the plan of the workout that earns the star) | no | `pushup-archer` | archer |
| mastered, but the person swapped the archer into the slot by hand | yes | half one-arm | archer (an explicit pick is exactly that exercise) |

Why:

- Every path that earns the fifth star (`achievedTiers` contains 5) also moves the route on: `tryAdvance` (the top tier branch) sets
  `active[family]` to the successor when its prerequisites are met, the level editor's "all five" is `SelfAssessment` above the top tier (moves on
  explicitly), and `moveOnByRule` does the same for rule exercises. The planner then takes `activeInFamily` as the exercise of the slot
  (`Planner.chooseFor`) and never plans the mastered one again.
- A plain hand-set level 5 ("I am at level 5") is not mastery: the fifth star is earned by the three good sessions at level 5, so the archer is
  correctly still planned. Level 5 is shown with four stars until then.
- The end screen's "Mastered" card is built from the replay that includes the session just finished. The workout that earns the fifth star was
  planned before it, with the archer in it. So a screenshot of the end screen of that workout shows "Mastered" for an exercise that was in the same
  workout. The next workout plans the half one-arm push-up. This is the most likely explanation of the owner's screenshots: the card appears in the
  workout that earned it.
- The only way a mastered exercise stays planned later is a deliberate pick: a swap in Preview or an added exercise (`explicitPick` in the planner).

## Not a design question, but worth saying

The owner could not tell this from the card ("Mastered" with nothing after it). That is the reason for L3.4: the card of a mastered exercise now shows
"Next: Half One-Arm Push-Up · Level 1" with its thumbnail when the route actually moved on to it (`ExerciseProgress.nextVariationId`, only set when
`activeInFamily` equals the route's next step, so the card never promises a step the planner will not use).

If the owner still sees the archer planned in a workout after a *later* session, the history is not one of the above (for example a swapped-in slot)
and the plan and history are needed to say more.
