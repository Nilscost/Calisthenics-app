# Owner check · R8 (0.11.0-r8, versionCode 14 · final build)

This is the phone walk-through for the new version. Automated checks and emulator screenshots do not cover real voice, haptics, screen-off timing or workout comfort. Exercise content remains DRAFT.

1. In the existing app, export a backup from Settings. Install `app-debug-R8.apk` as an update; keep the existing app installed. Check that History, levels, saved routines and equipment profiles remain present.
2. Check the selected equipment profile in Settings. Training exercises and programs must follow it. Search may still show library entries with their equipment requirements.
3. Train should stay compact, without Extras or extra pool switches. Fixed programs are under Routine. Add specialist exercises manually in Preview; equipment requirements still apply. A generated workout should not contain two exercises from the same progression.
4. In Workout preview, change a plain rest with −/+. The total should change, the rest/stretch card should share its row with the existing + button, and the chosen rest should carry into Start. Check this with larger system text too.
5. During a workout, listen for the slower voice and left/right announcements. A two-sided hold should give five seconds to change position without shortening either hold. Check pause/resume and briefly turn the screen off.
6. When an untried comparable alternative appears, its starting target should reflect the associated main exercise conservatively. For example, 12 feet-elevated push-ups suggests 8 diamond push-ups; 40-second side plank suggests 30 seconds for the knee-supported alternative. Existing alternative results and manual targets take precedence. Main mastery advances the path; alternative results stay separate. There is no need to create artificial workout history to test this.
7. Progress should show a simple main path. Open Other ways and Helps with, or All progressions for specialist routes. Search for Teaser to find V-up. Check that every exercise opens its own video.
8. Routine should offer eight fixed programs alongside saved routines, compact equipment/duration summaries, expandable details and the authored flow defaults. Inspect Army 4C without flow and an HSD/Pilates flow. Flow uses timed items without per-set rep logging; stop or finish it once and check the end screen.
9. Inspect videos from several movement groups, including the repaired wall-supported views and rear hand clasp. Check thoracic rotation for a natural shoulder and rear hand clasp for a clear view behind the back. The replacement neck, scapular, wrist, foot and Pilates videos should show the named action; report any unclear motion.
10. Check History, a correction, Progress and Settings in light/dark mode and at larger text. Give each main tab a 1–5 score, one thing that works well and one thing to change.

Phone results, sound/haptics and owner acceptance remain open until this walk-through is performed.
