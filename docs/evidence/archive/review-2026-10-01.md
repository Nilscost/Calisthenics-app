## Problems found (ordered by importance)
1. **Mac build will fail as shipped**: `local.properties` says `sdk.dir=/opt/android-sdk`
   (sandbox path). On the Mac delete it or point to `~/Library/Android/sdk`.
2. **Pass criteria disagree** between docs: runbook says each cue ≤ 1 s, ADR 0003 says < 5 s,
   instrumented test says < 4 s. Pick one before the run (recommend: spec A02 = 1 s per cue, 2 s total).
3. **G1 doesn't test spoken guidance or music ducking.** AUD-01 requires *offline English speech*
   and AUD-02 requires lowering music for cues. The spike only plays a beep with no audio focus.
   Either add a TTS + ducking check to the spike or explicitly split G1 into G1a (timer/beeps) and G1b (speech/ducking).
4. **First cue is probably silent**: `SoundPool.load()` is asynchronous and the first cue fires at t=0.
   The CSV still logs it, so it's not a data bug, but you may hear 4 not 5 beeps in the smoke run.
5. **Data-loss risk (later, T07/G5)**: `AppDatabase` uses `exportSchema=false` +
   `fallbackToDestructiveMigration()`. Any schema bump with a missing migration silently wipes history.
   The upstream migration test was deleted instead of fixed. Must be fixed before real use.
6. **Spike service is `exported=true` with no permission** — any app on the phone could start it.
   Acceptable for a one-day debug probe; must be deleted right after G1 (already planned).
7. Hygiene: duplicated `config/config/aboutlibraries/` committed to git; `versionName` still `0.1.0-t03`;
   ADR says S21 is Android 13/14 — it may be on Android 15 / One UI 7 (record actual version).
8. Clutter: three copies (`Calsthenics V1/`, `pkg/`, the 16 MB zip). Keep `pkg/calisthenics` as the single source.
9. 26-task plan, at task 4 after a week. Expect weeks of further work; a smaller MVP cut is worth considering after G1.
