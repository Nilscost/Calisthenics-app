# Owner phone check — Release R6 (library batch), version 0.9.0-r6

Install `Apps/builds/app-debug-R6.apk` (versionCode 9) over the existing app. R6 = R5 plus the fonts (Barlow Condensed and IBM Plex Sans), L11 to L13. The walk-through for the whole version is **`owner-check-final.md`**; this list is only what is new since R5. Nothing was tested on a phone by the implementer; screenshots: `docs/evidence/ui/R6-review.md`, `L11-review.md`, `L11b-review.md`, `L12-review.md`, `L13-review.md`.

1. **Fonts.** Titles, timers and numbers are narrow and heavy (Barlow Condensed), text is IBM Plex Sans. Readable in dark and with the large-font setting?
2. **Stretches (L11).** 24 stretches; deeper ones (pigeon, 90/90, frog, couch, butterfly, pancake, cobra, sleeper) are never put between sets, only picked by hand or used in a cool-down. Do the between-sets stretches feel gentle enough?
3. **Starting To Stretch (L11b).** Train, Routine, Starting To Stretch (or the upper or lower half): each stretch has 10 gentle bumps, then holds of 10, 20, 30 s. Is it clear what to do? About 20–25 minutes.
4. **Warm-up (L12).** With "Warm-up first" on, the blocks are labelled raise, activate, mobilise and the later items (arch hang, support hold, easier squat, easier hinge) appear only once you have reached the matching step. The wrist prep appears only on push days.
5. **Lower legs (L13).** Progress, Lower legs: calf raises (the loaded step uses your dumbbells) and wall tibialis raises. Do the clips help? (They are weak, see the clip review.)
