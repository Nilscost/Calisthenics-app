# Owner phone check — Release R3 (questionnaire, Train, Progress, progression switch), version 0.6.0-r3

Install `Apps/builds/app-debug-R3.apk` (versionCode 6). Nothing was tested on a phone by the implementer; screenshots: `docs/evidence/ui/R3-review.md`. (Owner decision: R1-R4 are phone-tested together at the end.)

1. **Train (doc 17 §2.1).** TODAY'S WORKOUT; OBJECTIVE with Body part · Skill buttons and a dropdown under them (Routine appears later, with the ready-made routines); EQUIPMENT as a dropdown (Home, Travel, New profile; the pencil edits the profile); SETS stepper; BETWEEN SETS Stretch | Rest; TIME and EXERCISES tiles; START WORKOUT starts directly, Workout preview opens the preview. Is it clear? Is anything you used to do on Train missing (the Reps/Timed style moved to Settings; the old Upper/Lower/Core toggles are now Body part choices)?
2. **Questionnaire (Settings -> Redo).** The header shows GOAL · EQUIPMENT · LEVEL with the current part highlighted, no dots. The goal page has the same Body part / Skill control. Each level page lists the whole progression of the family (e.g. push-ups up to the one-arm push-up) with a picture and the five levels of every exercise. "You are ready" shows stars for the levels below the one you chose.
3. **Progress (doc 17 §2.6).** PROGRESS, By type | By skill. The tree runs top to bottom (easier first); each exercise is a tile with its level number on the left and its picture on the right; mastered ones show a check and gold; branches split side by side. First tap = ring and a panel above the tabs; OPEN EXERCISE DETAIL opens the page with clip, body figure, five levels, unlocks, needs and cautions (no buttons). Do exercises below your current one show five stars?
4. **By skill.** Pick a skill (muscle-up, pistol, planche ...): the chain of exercises that leads to it.
5. **Automatic progression (Settings -> Progression).** On by default. Switch it off, do a workout that would earn a level: nothing moves. Switch it on again: levels follow your logged results as before.
6. **Stars after the questionnaire.** A start at level 3 shows two stars (D8: one kind of star).

Not checked by anyone yet: feel on the phone, haptics, sound, voice, screen-off behaviour.
