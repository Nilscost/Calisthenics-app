# Owner check · R8.2 (0.11.2-r8, versionCode 16)

Phone walk-through. Nothing here has been tried on a phone; content remains DRAFT. Install `app-debug-R8.2.apk` as an update over R8.1 (same signing certificate); export a backup first.

1. **G1 Power in categories.** Progress > By type has no Power tab. Open Squat: Jump (jump squat up to depth jump) and Bound (jumps, skater bound, single-leg hops) sit next to the strength routes. Push still has Power push-up. Depth jumps stay locked until box jumps are solid; power push-ups stay capped at 3 reps. In Preview the add sheet lists them under Squat (and Push); the automatic workout does not pick them.
2. **G2 Warm-up category.** Progress > By type > Warm-up lists the prehab and warm-up items grouped by Shoulder, Wrist, Hip, Knee, Neck and General warm-up; each opens its video. Turn the warm-up on for a push-up, dip or handstand session (and a pistol or single-leg session): the warm-up adds 2 to 4 matching items (wrist prep, band external rotation or Y raise, Spanish squat or knee item, clamshell) and is not longer than before.
3. **G3 Include row.** On Train, under Workout format, one row Include with Warm-up, Conditioning and Cool-down. A button is gold when in, outlined when out; the old switches are gone. Switch all three on and open the Preview: it shows the warm-up items, the conditioning finisher (minutes with - / +, remove an exercise) and the cool-down. Conditioning is 5 to 10 minutes of 30-second intervals without rest between exercises; check the exercises fit your equipment and level. Go back and return: the edits are still there. Start it and do the finisher.
4. **G4.** At the bottom of Train there is only the time tile; the exercises per round stepper is the one control and still changes the workout.

End with a score from 1 to 5 for Train, Progress, History and Settings, one thing that works well and one to change.
