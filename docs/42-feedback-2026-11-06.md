## What the owner reported

1. A **Half One-Arm Push-Up** appeared in the Workout preview and was too hard, while the owner is working on the Archer Push-Up.
2. Remove **"Too easy"** from the training screen; keep it only on the end screen next to "Too hard".
3. The end screen showed **"New exercise unlocked: Bulgarian Split Squat"** although Split Squat is not at its top level.
4. The end screen shows **two cards for one exercise** (the gold level-up card and the "Your exercises" card). Show one card per exercise, like Plank Shoulder Taps.
5. **Stars**: "Level 4 of 5" shows 3 gold stars, which reads as level 3. Show the current level's star as a black star with a gold outline, so the stars and the "N of 3 good sessions to Level N+1" line agree.
6. **Calf Raise** and **Plank Shoulder Taps** targets are too easy (shoulder taps come right after the plank).

## Causes found during review

- **(1)** `pushup-half-one-arm` is authored as an `ALTERNATE` at `pushup-archer` (`docs/29-progression-routes.json`, `role: ALTERNATE, atVariationId: pushup-archer`). Alternatives rotate with the main exercise across workouts (owner decision 2026-10-16), so the planner picks it in turn. It is not comparable to the archer push-up: it is the step towards the one-arm push-up, and its tier 1 is 8 reps per side.
- **(3)** `split-squat-bulgarian` has the prerequisite `split-squat` tier 3. `newExercises()` in `SessionProgressSummary.kt` announces every variation whose prerequisites this session newly met, including the next main step of the exercise the person is still training.
- **(4)** `EndProgressCards` renders `UnlockCard` for each level-up/mastery and then `ExerciseCard` for every exercise, so a leveled exercise appears twice.
- **(5)** `StarRow(stars)` lights only completed levels; the current level has no mark.
- **(6)** Current targets: calf raise 15/18/20/22/25, plank shoulder taps 6/8/10/12/16 (total taps).
