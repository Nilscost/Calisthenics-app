## Engine bugs (highest priority: T01–T04)

**F1 Wrong level after progress on a related exercise.** Owner: "feet-elevated push-up at 4 stars, and I get knee push-up". Also: "I did split squats and the engine put me at the first squat level again."
- **Expected:** the planner never prescribes an exercise below what the person has demonstrated on the same route. Achievements on a main step, or on an alternate at the same or a higher rung, count for the route position.
  - 4 stars on feet-elevated push-up (a rung above standard) means the push-up slot gets feet-elevated, diamond, or the next main step. Never knee or standard.
  - Split-squat work means the squat slot starts at split squat or above. Never assisted or air squat.
- Check how the current position is derived per route: anchor vs. main successor, alternate evidence, physical-family vs. logical-route ids, and the R8 import of legacy history.
- Tests: the two owner scenarios, plus a backup-imported history case.

**F2 Two exercises from one progression in a session.** Owner: knee push-up and standard push-up in the same workout. R8 claimed duplicate prevention.
- Find why it still happens. Likely knee is a route alternate or support, or in a different physical family or group, and the duplicate key misses it.
- **Rule:** one exercise per logical route per session, alternates and support included. The only exception is when the person adds one manually in Preview.
- Test with the owner's case.

**F3 Logged reps must be the actual reps.** Owner: inverted rows, logged 6, progression shows 10, the prescribed number.
- Evidence, stars, charts and History must use the logged value. A prescribed target counts only if the person accepts it unchanged (DONE without editing). Check every path: the in-workout logger, DONE in breaks, the end-screen editor, skip, and flow mode.
- Test: prescribe 10, log 6, then progression, chart and stars all see 6.

**F4 Level-up rule: 3 good sessions within 14 days.** Owner: "otherwise if you don't train 3 times a week you can never go up."
- **New rule:** an exercise's level (tier/star) rises after **3 qualifying sessions within any rolling 14-day window**. Qualifying means the target is met without Too hard or Pain. Sessions on the **same exercise or its same-rung alternates** count together (e.g. feet-elevated + diamond).
- Gaps longer than 14 days don't reset earned stars.
- Too easy keeps its one-tap jump (R7.2 F7, O1).
- Replace whatever the current "consecutive / per-week" rule is. Update the D-decision tests and record the changed assertions in the progress notes: owner decision 2026-10-17, superseding the earlier rule.
