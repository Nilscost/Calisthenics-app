# Privacy policy: Calisthenics Personal

Last updated: 2026-10-05

- **No account, no tracking.** The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting.
- **No network use.** The app does not connect to the internet. Exercise clips and the catalog are bundled in the app.
- **Your data stays on your phone.** Workout history, feedback, equipment and settings are stored in the app's private storage.
- **Sharing is your choice.** Data leaves the phone only when you use Export/Share. The file goes to the app you pick and nowhere else.
- **Notifications, sound and flashlight** are used only for the running workout (the flash can signal interval changes). The app does not record audio or take photos.
- **Deleting:** uninstall the app, or clear its storage, to remove everything.
- **Not medical advice.** Exercise carries risk. Stop if you feel pain.
