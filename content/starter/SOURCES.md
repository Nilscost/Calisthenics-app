# Starter catalog sources (DRAFT, researched 2026-10-04)

Status: nothing here is REVIEWED. Owner reviews at G2/G3.

## What was and was not checked
I could NOT read the books. I read book descriptions, tables of contents and reader/coach
reviews via web search, and nothing else. A source id on a variation therefore means
"this book is known to teach this movement or ladder", not "I verified page X".
Chapter/page checks are still open and are a reviewer task.
All rep/hold targets, work windows and recovery times are PRODUCT_HEURISTIC numbers I wrote
(`approved: false`). They are NOT copied from any book.

## The five books
| id | Book | Role in this catalog | Evidence seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| convict-conditioning-wade | Convict Conditioning, Paul Wade | Push-up, squat, bridge, pull-up ladders (10-step chains, beginner steps used) | Descriptions: six movements, ten-step chains. Reviews are split (some readers criticise it). |
| overcoming-gravity-2-low | Overcoming Gravity 2nd ed., Steven Low | Progression logic, rows, planks, mobility, skill graph | Author site + 3 reviews: ~600 pp, progression charts, "Methods of Progression" chapter. Not beginner-light but covers basics. |
| building-the-gymnastic-body-sommer | Building the Gymnastic Body, Christopher Sommer | Straight-body core, strength-hold ideas, stretch/mobility pairing | Table of contents + reviews: static holds (L-sit, planche, levers), weak on programming. |
| you-are-your-own-gym-lauren | You Are Your Own Gym, Mark Lauren | Beginner variations, no-equipment options | Reviews: 125+ exercises, easier/harder variants, 10-week programs. |
| never-gymless-enamait | Never Gymless, Ross Enamait | Home bodyweight variations | Only a short description and one review seen: weakest evidence of the five. |

Why these five: they appeared repeatedly in "best calisthenics books" results and cover the
exercises plus progression ladders requested. "Most famous" is my judgement from search
results, not a sales ranking.

## Deliberate omissions
Skill-level moves from the books (planche, front lever, handstand push-up, muscle-up, one-arm
work) are NOT in the starter pack: the plan excludes advanced inversions and the app has no
skill-goal planner yet (ADR B4). Add them as a later expansion pack.
